Remember Carlos

His smile. His laughter. His kindness. His memories.

Who Carlos Was

Carlos, lovingly known as “Chatito,” was a young person whose smile and personality left an impression on the people around him.

He loved being outdoors. He enjoyed playing football with the neighborhood kids and riding his bike with his friends.

Carlos loved talking to people. He didn't care about age differences or whether someone was already part of his circle. He wanted to be friends with everyone.

He was respectful, making eye contact and giving a firm handshake. People noticed his smile, his humor, and the way he could make people laugh.

What we want people to remember:

Carlos was more than the way his life ended. He was a son, a friend, a brother, a family member, and a person who brought his own unique light into the lives of others.

The Things We Remember

These are the qualities and memories we hope continue to live on through The Chatito Project.

His Smile

Carlos had a contagious smile that people noticed and remembered.

His Humor

He was funny, quick with comebacks, and knew how to make people laugh.

His Heart

Carlos wanted to connect with people and make friends with everyone around him.

Remembering Carlos

Carlos “Chatito” is laid to rest in Elgin, Texas.

Anyone who would like to honor Carlos is welcome to keep him in their thoughts, share a memory, talk to him, or simply spend a quiet moment remembering the person he was.

Carlos always enjoyed being the center of attention, so we like to imagine he would be happy knowing people are still talking about him, remembering his smile, and keeping his memory alive.

Come as you are. Talk to him. Tell him you love him. Share what's on your heart.

His story continues through everyone who remembers him. 💙
