THE CHATITO PROJECT

Carlos “Chatito”

Carlos had a contagious smile, a great sense of humor, and a way of making people feel welcome. He loved being outdoors, playing football with neighbors, riding his bike with friends, and talking to everyone. He wanted to befriend people regardless of age. His family wants people to know Carlos for the joy, humor, friendship, and love he shared.

You’re loved. You matter. You’re not a burden. Don’t give up.
The Chatito Project • Created in loving memory of Carlos “Chatito.”
