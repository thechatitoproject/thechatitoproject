Remembering Carlos

Carlos was known as “Chatito” to the people who loved him. He had a smile that could light up a room and a personality that made people feel welcome.

He loved being outdoors, playing football with the neighborhood kids, riding his bike with friends, talking to people, and making connections with everyone around him.

The Chatito Project exists so that Chatito's story can continue to make a difference.

You are loved. You matter. You are not a burden. Don't give up.

Why We Are Here

Sometimes someone is hurting in ways we cannot see. A conversation, a listening ear, or simply reminding someone that they matter can make a difference.

Speak Up

We encourage young people to talk about what they are feeling and to reach out when life feels overwhelming.

Listen & Connect

We believe listening without judgment can help someone feel heard, valued, and less alone.

Spread Hope

We want every young person to know that a difficult moment does not have to define their future.

THE CHATITO PROJECT • AUGUST 6, 2026

Back-to-School Event

On August 6, 2026, The Chatito Project hosted our first Back-to-School event, bringing our community together to help local families prepare for the upcoming school year.

202 Families Helped

We are incredibly grateful to everyone who donated, volunteered, supported, and helped make this event possible. What started as an idea to give back to our community became a meaningful day filled with kindness, generosity, and hope.

This event is just the beginning. We hope to continue growing, reaching more families, and creating events that bring our community together in honor of Chatito.
