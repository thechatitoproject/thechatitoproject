Remember Carlos

These photos are a small glimpse of the person Carlos was — his smile, his laughter, his personality, and the memories that will always remain.

Chatito was so funny. He loved making people laugh and could always find a way to bring a smile to someone's face.
One of our favorite things to do as a family was go to lakes, beaches, and pools. Something about the sound of the water brought us peace.
Chatito had the prettiest smile. That smile could light up a room.
He loved wearing sweaters, jackets, and hoodies—even in the blistering Texas summers. 😂
I’ll miss his hustle so much. He loved making his own money and would do any task he could to earn it. He was always looking for a way to make a few dollars.
That contagious smile once again. ❤️
A few pictures of Chatito and his daddy, Chato. ❤️
He was so tall. At only 13 years old, he was already 5'5".
That AirPod used to get me so annoyed. 😂 He would have it playing so loud!
Chatito loved to gamble. 😂 I believe this was the first time he played UNO. Once his big brother Steven taught him how to play, he tried to make bets with his sister because she didn’t know how to play. That was Chatito—always trying to find a way to make a bet!
That’s Chatito as a bear on Halloween. He always wanted to be close to me, his mommy. ❤️
I saved the best for last. Kimmie and Chatito were more than siblings—they were each other’s safe place. They were always together, and he was her protector. Whenever I see a decoration of a bear, I buy it and try to pair it with a bee. He was my bear, and she is my bee. 🐻🐝❤️

His smile. His laughter. His memories.

Carlos will always be remembered for the person he was, the love he shared, and the lives he touched.
